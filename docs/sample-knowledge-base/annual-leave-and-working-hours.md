# Acme Group — Annual Leave & Working Hours (Fictional Sample Document)

This is a fictional sample document created for a portfolio demo. "Acme Group" is not
a real company, and no real employee data appears anywhere in this file.

## Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of annual leave per year, in addition
to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on
their contracted hours.

## Booking Annual Leave

Annual leave is booked through the HR self-service portal under "Leave Requests."
Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance and require line
manager approval.

## Checking Remaining Leave

Employees can view their remaining annual leave balance at any time in the
"Leave Requests" section of the HR self-service portal.

## Standard Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9am–5:30pm, Monday to Friday, with flexible start
times between 8am and 10am under the flexible working policy.
