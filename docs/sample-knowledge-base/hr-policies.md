# Acme Group — HR Policies (Fictional Sample Document)

This is a fictional sample document created for a portfolio demo. "Acme Group" is not
a real company, and no real employee data appears anywhere in this file.

## Contacting HR

Employees can contact the Acme Group HR team through the internal HR helpdesk portal,
available Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm. For urgent matters, HR can also be reached by
internal chat during business hours.

## Employee Information Updates

Employees can update personal details such as address, emergency contacts and bank
details through the HR self-service portal under "My Details."

## Internal Opportunities

Internal job openings are posted on the Careers section of the intranet and are
visible to all employees. Employees who have been in their current role for at
least six months are encouraged to apply for internal transfers.

## Probation Period

New employees complete a standard probation period of three months, during which
regular check-ins are held with their line manager.
