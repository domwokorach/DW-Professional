# Acme Group — Payroll & Payslips (Fictional Sample Document)

This is a fictional sample document created for a portfolio demo. "Acme Group" is not
a real company, and no real payroll or employee data appears anywhere in this file.

## Payslips

Payslips are issued monthly and are available to view or download through the
Payroll section of the HR self-service portal, usually by the 25th of each month.

## Pay Dates

Salaries are paid on the last working day of each calendar month, directly into
the employee's registered bank account.

## Payroll Queries

Questions about payroll, tax codes or deductions can be directed to the Payroll
team via the internal Payroll query form, available on the intranet.

## Invoices & Expenses

Expense claims and supplier invoices are submitted through the internal Finance
portal. Approved expense claims are typically reimbursed within the next two
payroll cycles.
